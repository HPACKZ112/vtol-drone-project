# CAD and drawings

Add models and drawings once a geometry has been developed. Keep native editable
files with readable exports where useful; CAD exports are not globally ignored.

Record dimensions, material, mass estimate, centre-of-gravity reference, assembly
interfaces, rotor clearances, tilt travel, and mechanism loads. Tie each drawing
revision to its design decision and hardware configuration. No CAD is included yet.

The [owner's rough sketch](../../docs/personal-work.md) is the initial concept,
not CAD or a dimensioned drawing. The rear rotors are intended to mount on the
horizontal tail, and all four tilt forward. Develop rotor centres, battery/CG,
hinge geometry and the tail/boom load path before treating that layout as buildable.
