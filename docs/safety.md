# Safety and test readiness

This plan is incomplete until reviewed for the actual hardware, facilities, and
supervision. Preliminary sizing calculations do not establish flight readiness.
High-energy batteries, rotating propellers, and moving tilt mechanisms require
controlled procedures.

| Hazard | Control to define and verify | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unexpected motor start | Propellers removed during setup; documented arming and isolation procedure | Ground checklist and checks |
| Propeller failure or contact | Suitable rated test stand, barriers, exclusion area, and supervision | Reviewed test arrangement |
| Battery fault | Appropriate charging/storage procedure, inspection, and emergency response | Battery instructions and local procedure |
| Tilt actuator fault | Reviewed travel limits, loads, feedback, and failure response | Mechanism review and ground tests |
| Control or link failure | Defined failsafe behaviour and controlled validation | Configuration and test logs |
| Structural failure | Reviewed load cases, attachment design, and inspections | Calculations and inspection records |

## Gates before powered testing

Confirm configuration and wiring, define test objectives and abort criteria,
inspect hardware, confirm instrument limits, establish isolation and emergency
procedures, and obtain the relevant supervisor or facility review. Keep people
clear of rotating parts. Stop after unexplained behaviour and review evidence.

## Gates before flight

Complete the staged ground and simulation checks, review control authority and
failure handling, confirm a suitable test site and current local aviation rules,
and record the pilot, supervision, weather limits, exclusion area, and emergency
plan. This document provides no flight authorisation or operational procedure.

## Incident and near-miss record

Record date, configuration, event, immediate response, cause, corrective action,
and validation of that action. There are no incident records in this starter.
